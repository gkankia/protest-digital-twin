About This Project

Protest Digital Twin · Tbilisi, Georgia

This is a living digital twin of the protest space, capturing the presence and voices of those who showed up. Every figure on the map represents a real person who placed themselves here.

By clicking "Place Yourself on the Map", you leave a permanent mark — your figure, your age, and your words become part of the record of this moment in history.

The project was built to document collective presence and give every participant a voice that outlasts the protest itself.
